Technical log

This dashboard is for general wellness only. It is not a medical device, and wrist sensors (especially blood oxygen) can be inaccurate. Ranges follow published guidance (American Heart Association, CDC, US Physical Activity Guidelines) but are not a diagnosis. If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, or sudden weakness, call 911. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise plan.
